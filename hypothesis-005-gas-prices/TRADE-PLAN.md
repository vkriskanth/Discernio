# Trade Plan: $10k Refining-Strain Play (Simple Version)

*Written 2026-07-18. Hypothetical exercise, per repo convention — not
advice. Full reasoning in [ANALYSIS.md](ANALYSIS.md).*

## The idea in one paragraph

Diesel is genuinely scarce (refinery closures + Russia's export ban + war),
but everyone knows it — refiner stocks are at 52-week highs. So: buy a
starter position in the highest-quality names now, save most of the money
for the pullback that history says is coming within weeks, and let winter
heating demand be the payoff. Sell if the scarcity actually ends.

## Step 1 — Today: buy $3,500

One ticket: **$3,500 of CRAK** (refiner ETF).
Or three tickets: **$1,500 VLO + $1,000 MPC + $1,000 PSX**.

Keep the remaining $6,500 in cash. Write down what you paid.

## Step 2 — The dip buy: $4,500 more, only if BOTH are true

1. **The stocks dropped ~10%** from your Step-1 price.
2. **Diesel is still scarce** — the weekly EIA report still shows distillate
   inventories roughly 8% or more below the 5-year average.

Then buy: **$3,500 more of the same names + $1,000 of PBF** (the high-risk,
high-reward one — it only ever gets bought on this dip, never at highs).

If no dip happens by Sept 30, keep the cash. Don't chase.

## Step 3 — Selling rules (in priority order)

| Rule | If this happens | Do this |
|------|-----------------|---------|
| Thesis over | EIA shows distillate inventories rebuilding toward normal **two Wednesdays in a row** AND Russia's diesel ban lapses (decision due 7/31) | Sell everything |
| Safety stop | Total position down **18%** | Sell everything, no debate |
| Take profit | Refining margins spike again (news says "record crack spreads", roughly 3-2-1 > $85) | Sell PBF and half the rest |
| Time's up | March 2027 arrives | Sell whatever's left |

Otherwise: hold through December — winter is the catalyst.

## The 10-minute Wednesday routine

Every Wednesday after 10:30am ET, check the EIA weekly report
([eia.gov/petroleum/supply/weekly](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/supply/weekly/)):

- **Distillate inventories vs 5-year average** — this one number is the
  whole thesis. Still 8%+ below = thesis alive. Two straight weeks of
  rebuilding = get out.
- Glance at your position vs the Step-2 and Step-3 triggers. Done.

Also on the calendar: **7/31** Russia ban expiry/renewal (news will cover
it), **late July** refiner earnings (if stocks *fall* on great earnings,
that's your Step-2 dip forming — good news, not bad).

## What NOT to do

- **No futures** — one contract is ~$170k of exposure, 17x this account.
- **No options** — you'd pay record volatility prices on top of record
  stock prices.
- **No gasoline plays (UGA)** — gasoline demand dies in winter; diesel is
  the scarce molecule.
- **No shorting** anything here — the tail risks (hurricanes, cold winter)
  are all against shorts.
- **No adding beyond the plan** — $8,000 max in the market, ever.

## What to expect

| How it plays out | Roughly |
|------------------|--------:|
| Dip comes, you buy it, winter is tight | **+$800–900** |

Modest upside, capped downside. That's the honest shape of betting on a
true story that the market already knows.
