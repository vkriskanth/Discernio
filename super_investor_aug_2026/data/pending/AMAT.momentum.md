# AMAT — Momentum Read

## Thesis

AMAT's tape is a textbook late-cycle momentum name: +204.9% over 12 months, +77.8% over 6, +31.5% over 3, but the last month rolled over -8.4% and RSI14 has cooled from what was almost certainly overbought territory to a neutral 52 — this reads as a monster AI-capex-driven run now digesting, not a fresh breakout. Trading 41.6% above its 200DMA with a 1.62 beta, the stock is priced for perfection and any market-wide de-rating in semicap names lands with amplified force. Positioning offers no cushion on the way down: short interest is a token 2.11% of float with only 1.34 days to cover, so there's no squeeze fuel to buy against a drawdown, and the 1.21 put/call OI ratio reads more as longs quietly hedging gains after a 200% run than as smart money pressing a fresh bearish bet. Egerton's brand-new 3.64%-of-portfolio initiation in Q2 2026 and Tiger Global's 85% share add in Q1 2026 confirm real institutional conviction chasing the trend right into the highs — supportive of persistence, but it also means the crowd buying is exposed to the same air pocket with nothing underneath to absorb it. Net: this is a trend to respect, not chase at current levels — the setup favors adding on a pullback toward the 200DMA over paying up here.
