# AMZN — Momentum Note (as of 2026-08-12)

**Thesis**
AMZN's trend is intact and reaccelerating, not extended: the stock chopped
sideways for three months (ret_3m +0.80%) before the tape turned in the last
month (+8.27%), and the 6- and 12-month returns (+29.47%, +21.08%) confirm the
underlying uptrend never broke — price sits 12.9% above its 200DMA with RSI14
at 68.6, warm but short of textbook overbought. Positioning offers no squeeze
tailwind: short interest is negligible (1.0% of float, 2.1 days to cover), so
this isn't short-covering fuel, it's a market with essentially no bearish bet
to unwind — upside has to come from fresh buying and downside has no
short-covering cushion either. Options skew leans constructive (put/call OI
0.54, more calls open than puts), consistent with dealers positioned for
continued upside rather than hedging a top. The superinvestor tape is the
strongest input here: sixteen managers added or initiated in Q1/Q2 2026,
several at real conviction size — Tepper +98% shares to 7.5% of book, Hong
+57% to 5.3%, Klarman +47% to 4.1%, Ackman +19% to 2.8% — spanning macro,
value, and activist styles, which reads as broad-based smart-money
confirmation of the technical trend rather than late-cycle chasing. Net: this
is a resumption pattern with fundamental sponsorship behind it, not a blow-off.

breakout, and a beta-amplified reversal would hit with no short base to
