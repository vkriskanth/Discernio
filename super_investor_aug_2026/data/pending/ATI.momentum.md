# ATI — Momentum Analysis

## Thesis

ATI is in a full-blown melt-up: +206% trailing 12 months, with the trend still accelerating rather than fading (+77% over 6m, +40% over 3m, +21% in just the last month), and price sits 55.5% above its 200DMA with RSI14 at 73 — extended but not yet climactic. This is not a short-covering rally: short interest is a thin 4.2% of float at 2.15 days to cover, and the put/call OI ratio of 0.63 shows options positioning tilted bullish rather than defensively hedged, so the move is being carried by conviction longs, not squeezed bears — which means there's little forced-buying cushion left and no short base to absorb a reversal. Viking Global (Q1 2026) and Polen Capital (Q2 2026) both initiated new positions, but at modest ~1.3-1.4% portfolio weights — read this as thematic/tactical participation in the aerospace-titanium supercycle narrative, not a max-conviction valuation call, especially since Polen's entry lands after most of the run already happened. Fundamentals offer zero cushion for the tape: 66.6x trailing P/E, 36.5x EV/EBITDA, and a 1.07% FCF yield mean the stock is priced for flawless execution, so the momentum here is a pure re-rating/multiple-expansion story riding on 10.6% revenue growth, not earnings catching up to price. Net: trend is genuinely strong and institutionally corroborated, but the combination of 200DMA extension, overbought RSI, and richly-priced fundamentals with no short base to buffer a stumble makes this a high-quality trend that is also a high-fragility one.

## Key risk

The stock is priced at 66x earnings and 36.5x EV/EBITDA with a ~1% FCF yield and sits 55% above its 200DMA after a 206% trailing-year run — any deceleration in the aerospace/defense titanium demand narrative (a guidance miss, program delay, or sector rotation out of high-multiple industrials) has no valuation floor and no short base to cushion the unwind, so a crack likely mean-reverts fast toward the 200DMA as momentum longs — including the recently-initiated, modestly-sized superinvestor positions — de-gross into the same air pocket that took the stock up.
