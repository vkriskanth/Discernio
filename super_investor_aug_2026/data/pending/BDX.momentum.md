# BDX — Momentum Note

## Thesis

BDX's tape is extreme, not merely strong: RSI14 sits at 85.1 and price is 14.7% above the 200DMA, while the return shape shows this is almost entirely a one-month event — the trailing month alone contributed 17.2% of a 16.2% three-month return, which means the three months before that were actually flat-to-down (6m return of 10.4% is *below* the 3m return of 16.2%). That's a gap that hasn't been tested, not a grinding trend. Options positioning confirms the crowd has already piled in behind the move (put/call OI 0.33, options_skew component 77.9), but short interest is thin (4.3% of float, 3.7 days to cover), so there's no squeeze fuel left to mechanically extend it — any continuation has to come from fresh buyers, not short capitulation. Greenhaven Associates' brand-new position sized at 9.93% of portfolio is a genuine conviction signal, but it's a Q2 2026 13F landing with the standard ~45-day reporting lag, so it almost certainly predates this run-up and reads as a value re-rating call (forward P/E 13.3 vs. trailing 30.6, against -9.5% revenue growth and 6.6% ROE) rather than confirmation of a live momentum trade; it argues for a valuation floor on a pullback, not for chasing the current print. Net: the fundamental buyer showed up before the technical extension, leaving this tape more exposed to mean reversion over the next few weeks than the composite score suggests.

## Key risk

The trailing-month gap (essentially all of the 3-month return) stalls or partially reverses once the momentum-chasing buyers implied by the bullish options skew stop showing up — with short interest too thin (3.7 days to cover) to force a squeeze-driven continuation, there's no positioning mechanism to keep the move going once it loses its own bid, and the one ostensibly bullish fundamental data point (Greenhaven's 9.93%-of-portfolio buy) is a lagging 13F disclosure whose actual entry price and current conviction at today's level are unknown. Separately, `beta` is null in the packet, which hides how much of this move is market-beta-driven versus idiosyncratic — without it, the size of the drawdown risk in a broader market pullback can't be estimated from this data alone.
