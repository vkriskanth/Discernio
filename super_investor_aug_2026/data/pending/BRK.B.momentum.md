trades made and reported with a lag), so if BRK.B has already re-rated or
extended meaningfully above its 200DMA since these managers established their
adds, this read would be chasing stale conviction into an already-crowded,
technically extended print with no short-interest or options data to warn of
it — and conversely, if the buying was itself a response to a drawdown, the
"momentum" here could really be value investors catching a falling knife
rather than confirmation of an uptrend.
