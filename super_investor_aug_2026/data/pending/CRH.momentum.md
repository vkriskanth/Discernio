## CRH — Momentum Note (as of 2026-08-12)

**Thesis**

CRH's momentum has flipped from a positive multi-year run into an active
breakdown: the 6-month return of -21.6% against a 12-month return of only
-6.8% means the stock was up roughly high-teens over the first half of that
window before giving it all back and then some in the back half, and it's
still sitting 12.4% below its 200DMA. RSI14 at 51.67 — neutral rather than
oversold — despite that drawdown is the tell that the 1-month print (-5.5%,
a deceleration versus the -12.7% 3-month pace) reflects exhausted selling
more than a confirmed base, so this reads as a pause inside a downtrend, not
a reversal. Positioning offers no tailwind either way: short interest is a
thin 2.4% of float with only 3.53 days to cover, so there's no squeeze fuel
to catalyze a snapback, and the put/call OI ratio at ~1.01 is essentially
flat, showing no options-market conviction on direction. The two
superinvestor moves — Egerton Capital adding 58.87% to an existing stake and
Durable Capital initiating fresh — both landed while the stock was falling
(Q1-Q2 2026), which is a valuation-driven signal, not a momentum one; it
argues these managers are underwriting the balance-sheet/cash-flow story
(forward P/E 14.9x, 15.8% ROE) ahead of price confirmation, which can mean
early and a while underwater before it means right.

**Score adjustment**

adjusted_score: 29 — shade down a few points from the packet's 33.4; the
trend and vs_200dma components (37.0 and 25.2) already flag weakness, but
the composite doesn't fully capture that this is a trend *reversal* (strong
first-half gains erased) rather than a steady grind, and the neutral RSI is
more consistent with a stalled downtrend than a bottom, so I wouldn't let
the deceleration in the 1-month number or the superinvestor buying (a value
signal, not a price-momentum one) pull the score up.

**Key risk**

No squeeze fuel to arrest a renewed leg down: with short interest at just
2.4% of float and 3.53 days to cover, there's no crowded short base to force
covering if sellers return, so if the neutral RSI reading is a dead-cat
bounce rather than a base, price can keep drifting through the 200DMA gap
unimpeded — and the superinvestor buying, landing before any price
confirmation, offers no floor if the value thesis takes longer to play out
than the momentum trade can tolerate.
