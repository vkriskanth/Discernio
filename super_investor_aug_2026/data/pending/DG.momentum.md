# DG — Momentum Note

**Thesis**

DG's twelve-month tape nets to a flat +4.7%, but the path underneath is the real story: a brutal six-month air pocket (-18.2%) followed by a sharp three-month reclaim (+14.7%), now cooling with a -3.3% pullback over the past month. RSI14 at 58 and price sitting essentially on the 200DMA (-2.9%) put this squarely in "testing the trendline" territory — no overbought excess, no confirmed breakout, just a name deciding whether the 200-day flips from resistance to support. Positioning offers little squeeze fuel: short interest is thin at 3.9% of float with only 3.1 days to cover, so the bounce is being driven by fundamental buyers rather than shorts capitulating, and the call-tilted put/call OI (0.75) confirms modestly constructive but unstretched options sentiment. The superinvestor tape backs the reversal read — Pzena, a deep-value shop, added 48% to an already-material 1.1%-of-portfolio stake in Q2, the kind of sizing-up that signals conviction bought into the drawdown, while FPA's new 0.02%-of-portfolio starter and Ruane Cunniff's ~0%-weight add are too small to carry independent signal. Beta of 0.235 keeps this an idiosyncratic, company-specific story rather than a macro-momentum play — a defensive discount-retailer turnaround thesis, not a high-beta momentum trade. Net: constructive but unconfirmed — a stock that has stopped falling and is attracting value capital, but hasn't yet proven the bounce is more than a bounce.
