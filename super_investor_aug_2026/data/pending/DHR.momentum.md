# DHR — Momentum Desk Note

**Thesis:** DHR is a low-beta (0.80) defensive name mid-reversal, not mid-trend: the
tape has clawed back a -6.9% six-month drawdown with a sharp +23.1% three-month
rally, leaving price essentially pinned at the 200DMA (+0.67%) and the 12-month
return flat (-0.16%) — this is a base-and-recover pattern, not a breakout.
RSI14 at 66.8 sits just under overbought, meaning most of the easy reversion
gain from the 6-month low is likely already captured and the setup has limited
room before mean-reversion risk dominates. Positioning offers little
mechanical tailwind: short interest is negligible (1.92% of float, 1.9 days to
cover) so there is no squeeze fuel, though the sub-1.0 put/call OI ratio
(0.57) shows options flow leaning bullish. The more interesting signal is
fundamental-investor corroboration — Maverick Capital and FPA opened new
positions in consecutive quarters (Q1 and Q2 2026), joined by adds from
Dorsey, Van Den Berg, Weitz, and Third Point, a cluster of quality/value
managers accumulating through the same window as the price recovery, which
argues the rally has a valuation floor under it rather than being pure
technical drift. Net: this reads as a stabilizing, low-volatility name being
re-rated by patient capital, with the momentum score capped by the fact that
the "uptrend" is really just a recovery to flat.

**Score adjustment:** `adjusted_score: 56`

the name back below its trendline and re-open the 6-month drawdown, and with
short interest this low there's no forced-covering bid to cushion the
downside.
