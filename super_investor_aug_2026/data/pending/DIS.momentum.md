# DIS — Momentum Note

## Thesis

DIS is a sharp one-month reversal (+7.9%) sitting on top of a stock that's still down 6.5% over 12 months and only 1.3% above its 200DMA — the tape has flipped but the trend hasn't been re-established yet, and RSI14 at 75.5 says the bounce has already run hot into overbought territory with limited fuel left before a pullback. There's no short base to squeeze (1.13% of float, 1.78 days to cover), so this isn't a positioning-driven melt-up; the modestly bullish put/call skew (0.67) reflects genuine directional conviction rather than crowded shorts capitulating. What stands out is the superinvestor tape: over a dozen value managers piled in across both Q1 and Q2 2026 — Duan Yongping/H&H doubled down twice (+112%, then +12.9%), Pzena added 879%, and Tweedy Browne and First Pacific initiated new positions — buying into a name still red on the year, which is a classic value-crowd-buys-weakness signal rather than a momentum chase. That accumulation gives the recent pop a fundamental floor and lowers the odds of a full round-trip back to the lows, but it doesn't change the fact that the technical setup (overbought RSI, barely-positive 200DMA, still-negative 6/12m trend) is fragile and unconfirmed. Net: constructive but not clean — the smart-money accumulation argues for buying dips rather than chasing the current overbought print.
