# FRPT — Momentum Note

**Thesis.** FRPT's tape is a sharp, recent re-rating rather than a grinding trend: +35.6% over 1 month and +37.0% over 3 months dwarf the +4.0% 6-month and +13.7% 12-month returns, meaning almost the entire year's gain arrived in the last few weeks — consistent with Engaged Capital's freshly disclosed 8.87%-of-portfolio activist stake acting as the catalyst rather than a durable operating trend (Maverick's 356% "add" is noise at 0.02% of its book). RSI14 at 76.6 and price 17.5% above the 200DMA put the stock deep in overbought territory, and short interest at 19.2% of float with 6.68 days to cover means a real slice of this move is short covering, not fresh conviction buying — squeeze fuel that burns fast once it's spent. Options positioning is roughly balanced (put/call OI 0.84) with no visible downside hedging skew, so the market isn't yet pricing mean-reversion risk even though the setup screams stretched. Forward PE of 37x against a trailing 18.8x prices in a margin inflection that hasn't shown up yet — op margin is still just 7.1% — so the activist narrative (cost discipline, capital allocation) is doing more work than the fundamentals currently support. Net: real catalyst, real short-covering fuel, but a move this fast into this overbought a reading is more likely to consolidate or partially round-trip than extend cleanly.
