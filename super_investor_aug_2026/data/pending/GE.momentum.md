## Thesis

GE Aerospace's 12-month tape is real and freshly reaccelerated: after round-tripping roughly -6% in the spring (implied by ret_3m of 23.4% exceeding ret_6m of 16.2%), shares ripped higher over the trailing quarter to sit 15.6% above the 200DMA, with RSI14 at 61.7 leaving room before overbought territory kicks in. The 1-month print of just 3.7% shows that sprint cooling off its hot pace — the first crack in an otherwise clean trend structure. Positioning offers no combustible fuel either way: short interest is a token 1.4% of float at 3.6 days to cover, so this isn't a squeeze candidate and there's no smart-money short conviction to fade, while the put-heavy 1.22 OI skew reads more as profit-protection by longs after a big run than genuine bearish positioning. Polen Capital's fresh 2.22%-of-portfolio stake in Q2 is the signal that matters here — a concentrated quality-growth shop putting real size behind a name generating 48% ROE and 21% revenue growth validates the move as fundamentals-led rather than pure chart-chasing, even as Century Management's and Torray's token additions add little conviction weight of their own. The risk sitting underneath all of it is valuation: 43x trailing earnings, 34x EV/EBITDA, and a 1.7% FCF yield leave almost no cushion, and at 1.37 beta this name will amplify any market-wide derating faster than the technicals alone suggest.
