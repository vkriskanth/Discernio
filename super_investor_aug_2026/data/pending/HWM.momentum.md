## Thesis

HWM's headline 12-month return of 55.3% masks a trend that has already downshifted hard: the roughly 30% move packed into the March-to-June window has gone essentially flat over the last quarter (ret_3m 3.5%, ret_1m 3.0%), with RSI14 at a neutral 58.1 confirming a stall rather than a blow-off top. Price still sits 18.2% above the 200DMA, so the tape hasn't broken down, but there's no short base to cushion a reversal — short interest is a negligible 2.0% of float with only 2.3 days to cover, meaning any air pocket trades on real sellers rather than covering shorts. The put/call OI ratio of 1.47 skews unusually defensive for a name still up double digits, reading more as longs buying protection/collars into a stretched valuation (PE 60.6x, EV/EBITDA 41.7x, FCF yield just 1.1%) than as fresh bearish conviction. Polen Capital's brand-new buy_new position (1.06% of portfolio, Q2 2026) is a quality-growth manager paying up for 24.1% revenue growth and 34.7% ROE, not a value entry with margin of safety — it confirms the fundamental story but supplies no new momentum catalyst and no cushion if the multiple compresses. Net: this is a strong trailing-twelve-month winner that has lost its short-term legs, trading rich, unhedged by short-covering support, with the smart-money buy best read as a confirmation flag rather than a fresh catalyst.
