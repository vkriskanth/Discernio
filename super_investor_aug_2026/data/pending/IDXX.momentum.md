## IDXX — Momentum Note

**Thesis**
IDXX is a high-multiple, high-beta diagnostics name working through a multi-quarter derate — 12-month return of -8.2% and 6-month of -11.0% have it still 7.6% below the 200DMA — but the tape has stabilized: RSI14 sits at a neutral 53 and the 1-month print is back to +2.5% with 3-month roughly flat, consistent with a base-building phase rather than continued breakdown. Positioning offers no real tailwind or headwind: short interest is thin at 2.8% of float with only 3.2 days to cover, so there's no squeeze fuel, and the 0.91 put/call OI ratio is close to neutral with a mild call lean, arguing against reading options flow as a bearish tell. The standout signal is fundamental-investor conviction — Jensen Investment Management's Q2 2026 add lifted its position roughly 29x to 1.3% of portfolio, a real fresh-money commitment layered on top of 74% ROE, 35% operating margin, and 9.7% revenue growth that the price action hasn't yet rewarded (Oakmark's Q1 add, by contrast, is a rounding error at 0.0% of portfolio and shouldn't move the read). Valuation remains rich at 40x trailing / 34x forward earnings and 29x EV/EBITDA, so this isn't a value floor — it's a quality name whose stock has lagged its fundamentals and is now showing early signs of a momentum inflection. Net: this reads more like a stabilizing base with a credible catalyst (institutional conviction buying) than a name still trending down.

**Key risk**
The stock is still below its 200DMA and in a 6-12 month downtrend that hasn't technically reversed — at 1.54 beta and 40x/34x P/E, any broader growth/healthcare multiple compression or market pullback could resume the slide and turn this "stabilization" read into a bull trap, since low short interest also means there's no forced-buying mechanism to cushion a renewed leg down.
