## INTU — Momentum Note

**Thesis**

INTU is a classic bounce-in-a-downtrend setup: the 1-month +17.7% thrust looks impressive in isolation but sits on top of a stock down 20.5% over six months and 52.4% over twelve, and price remains 26% below its 200DMA — the longer trend hasn't turned, it's a stretched rubber band. RSI14 at 73.9 confirms the short-term move is overbought, raising mean-reversion odds into the next few weeks rather than continuation. Positioning offers little squeeze fuel: short interest is just 5.1% of float with only 2.8 days to cover, so this isn't short-covering panic driving the pop, and put/call OI at 1.06 shows options traders are still mildly hedged rather than chasing calls. The superinvestor activity cuts toward a value read, not a momentum one — Yacktman built a new stake in Q1 and added aggressively (+6561% shares) in Q2, joined by Van Den Berg and Hillman Capital initiating fresh positions, all classic value shops buying into weakness rather than strength, which is contrarian conviction, not confirmation of a trend change. Forward PE of 12x against a trailing 20x implies the Street expects an earnings inflection, but until price reclaims the 200DMA the tape itself hasn't validated that thesis. Treat the 1-month pop as a relief rally inside a still-intact downtrend until it clears the 200DMA on volume.

**Key risk**

The bounce doesn't mean-revert — it becomes the real inflection. If price clears the 200DMA on volume, trend-following flows could pile onto the already-hot 1-month move, and the value buyers (Yacktman et al.) turn out to have front-run the turn rather than caught a falling knife, invalidating the bearish/mean-reversion read entirely.
