## Thesis

JBS is inflecting off a soft patch, not riding a clean trend: the 1-month tape is up 11.4%, but that's a bounce inside a -6.8% 3-month and -11.9% 6-month drawdown that's left the stock still 3.7% under its 200DMA, netting out to a flat +0.6% over 12 months. RSI14 at 60.8 confirms the bounce has legs without being overbought, so there's room to run before a momentum trader has to worry about exhaustion. Positioning reads as squeeze fuel rather than a bearish signal: 14.9% of float short with 6.9 days to cover is a heavy load for a defensive packaged-foods name, and the options market isn't hedging against it — a 0.053 put/call OI ratio is about as one-sided bullish as skew gets, meaning shorts are exposed with almost no put protection sitting behind them if the rally extends. That positioning lines up with the fundamental story: two value-oriented shops, Davis Advisors and Dodge & Cox, both added aggressively (+121% and +90% shares across Q1-Q2 2026 for Davis, +164% for Dodge & Cox in Q1) into a name trading at 12.2x trailing / 8.9x forward earnings — this isn't momentum chasing, it's value conviction arriving just as price technicals turn, which is the combination that tends to trap shorts. The catch is the balance sheet: 283% debt-to-equity against a 3.1% operating margin means the squeeze narrative has no fundamental cushion if the recovery stalls.
