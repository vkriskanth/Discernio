# KKR — Momentum Read (as of 2026-08-08)

## Thesis

KKR is round-tripping off an oversold stretch: the trailing 12-month return is
still -28.4%, but the trend has clearly inflected, with 1-month +6.8%,
3-month +2.7%, and 6-month +4.1% all positive and accelerating into the print.
RSI14 at 61 confirms fresh buying pressure without being overbought, yet price
is still 3.5% under the 200DMA, so this reads as an early-stage reclaim
attempt rather than a confirmed uptrend — consistent with the mechanical
score of 39.5, which is dragged down by a soft 40.7 trend sub-score and 43.0
vs-200DMA sub-score. Positioning offers no extra tailwind: short interest is
thin at 2.2% of float with only 3.3 days to cover, so there's no squeeze fuel
sitting behind the move, and put/call open interest at 1.17 shows options
desks still running a mildly defensive skew rather than chasing the bounce.
What elevates this above a plain technical bounce is the sponsorship —
ValueAct built a brand-new 5.3%-of-portfolio position and Lindsell Train a
fresh 2.3% stake, both concentrated, patient-capital buys that typically
precede multi-quarter re-ratings rather than trades — while Ariel's 66% add
is directionally confirming but too small (0.18% of book) to move the needle
on its own. Net, fundamental conviction from quality-oriented managers is
arriving just as the technical picture stops deteriorating, but the tape
itself hasn't yet earned a high-momentum score.

## Key risk

Failed reclaim / value-trap bounce: if price can't close the -3.5% gap to the
200DMA and RSI rolls over from 61, the 1-month +6.8% pop is just a bounce
inside a still-intact 12-month downtrend (-28.4%). At beta 1.786, with
options desks already skewed toward puts (put/call OI 1.17), a broader market
pullback would hit KKR disproportionately hard and could stall the
superinvestor-driven re-rating well before it shows up in the price action.
