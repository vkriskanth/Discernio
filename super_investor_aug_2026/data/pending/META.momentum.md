# META — Momentum Note

## Thesis

META's price momentum is broken across every window — 1m -10.7%, 3m -3.9%,
6m -8.5%, 12m -23.7% — and the shape matters more than the levels: the
1-month return is worse than the 3-month, meaning the tape took a fresh leg
down in just the last few weeks rather than grinding out a stable base, and
the stock still sits 5.3% below its 200DMA with RSI14 pinned at 49.9 — dead
neutral, no oversold spring loaded. Short interest is negligible (1.43% of
float, 1.55 days to cover), so there's no squeeze fuel underneath this name;
any rally has to be built on fresh buying, not shorts capitulating. The one
genuinely bullish technical signal is options positioning — a put/call OI
ratio of 0.49 skews call-heavy, which diverges from the negative spot
trend and suggests derivatives desks aren't pricing further downside with
conviction. Layered on top is an unusually broad and persistent
superinvestor bid: Sosin, Ainslie, Loeb, Viking and Dodge & Cox all
initiated new positions across Q1-Q2 2026, and rather than being one-off dip
buys, several re-upped hard into the weakness — Viking added another 75.8%
on top of its new position, Tepper +54.6%, Ackman +20.1%, Dorsey +23.6% —
which reads as conviction accumulation through a drawdown, not a single
capitulation-buy. Net: this is a fundamentals-driven, multi-manager
accumulation story riding on top of a still-deteriorating price trend, not
a name with its own technical tailwind yet.

RSI neutral offering no oversold cushion and short interest too thin to
supply squeeze fuel, the pure price-momentum read is weaker than the blend
suggests, so the score should shade down toward the low-30s.

## Key risk

The superinvestor buying is a slow-money, multi-quarter signal that can be
early: if the 1-month air-pocket reflects a structural re-rating (e.g.
capex/margin worries) rather than a one-off reset, price and fundamentals
could keep falling together for several more quarters even as the buyer
list keeps growing, leaving momentum-following capital nothing to catch
until the trend actually inflects.
