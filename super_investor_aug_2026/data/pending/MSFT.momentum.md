# MSFT — Momentum Note

**Thesis:** MSFT is in the middle of a sharp V-shaped re-rating rather than a
clean trend — the stock is still down 4.6% over 12 months but has ripped
+20-28% across the 1/3/6-month windows, putting it 14.4% above its 200DMA
with RSI14 at 86.6, a genuinely extreme overbought reading rarely sustained
without a consolidation or pullback. Positioning offers no squeeze fuel to
extend the move mechanically: short interest is just 1.1% of float with only
2.17 days to cover, so this is a flow/fundamentals-driven advance, not a
short-covering rally, and the put/call OI ratio of 0.85 shows only mild
options-market bullishness rather than aggressive call-side chasing. The
superinvestor tape is the strongest pillar of the case — Ackman's Pershing
Square built a new 15.26%-of-portfolio anchor position in Q1 2026 and added
again in Q2, ShawSpring opened a fresh 7.3% stake in Q2, and a wide bench of
value-oriented managers (Dodge & Cox, Wedgewood, RV Capital, Oakmark,
Maverick) kept adding into Q2 2026, evidence that conviction broadened and
persisted through the run rather than being a single-quarter spike. Because
13F prints lag price action by weeks to months, though, much of this buying
likely predates the RSI 86.6 extension, so it validates the re-rating thesis
without confirming that fresh capital is still chasing the stock at current
levels. Net: durable accumulation underpins the move, but the technical
picture is stretched enough that a near-term air pocket is the higher-
probability path before the trend re-asserts.
