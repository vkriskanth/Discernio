# NFLX — Momentum Note

## Thesis

NFLX is a name where the trend and the tape are telling different stories: the
stock is down 35.1% over 12 months and still sitting 12.4% below its 200DMA,
yet it's up 5.2% over the past month with RSI14 at 73 — an overbought
short-term reading stacked on top of unrepaired longer-term technical damage,
the signature of a counter-trend bounce rather than a resumed uptrend. Crucially,
this bounce isn't short-covering fuel: short interest is just 2.2% of float
with only 1.9 days to cover, so there's no squeeze mechanics behind the move —
it's being bought, not forced higher. Options positioning confirms real
demand, with a put/call OI ratio of 0.49 skewing call-heavy. What tips this from
"overbought bounce, fade it" to "accumulation, respect it" is the buyer list:
eleven buy/add filings across Q1 and Q2 2026 from a genuinely diverse,
fundamentals-first bench — Ackman initiating at 4.8% of portfolio, Terry Smith
and Nygren both new/adding, Russo, Weitz, Bloomstran, Gayner all stepping in —
which reads as multi-style conviction that the 12-month de-rating overshot
rather than a broken thesis. Near-term, though, the setup argues for patience
on entry: RSI 73 with price still under the 200DMA leaves this vulnerable to a
give-back of the 1-month gain before any durable trend repair, and the 3-month
return (-10.1%) shows the underlying downtrend was still active as recently as
this quarter.

## Score adjustment

adjusted_score: 46

The mechanical score (38.6) is dragged down almost entirely by trend (33.9)
and vs-200DMA (25.3) components that treat the 12-month decline as fully
persistent, but it under-weights the bullish options skew (67.1) and misses
entirely that the buying pressure is institutional accumulation across two
quarters rather than short covering — that combination justifies nudging the
composite up into the high-40s, not the high-50s, given RSI is genuinely
overbought and the longer trend hasn't turned yet.

## Key risk

The 1-month bounce is a relief rally inside a still-intact downtrend (price
below 200DMA, 3-month return negative) with RSI already overbought and no
short-interest cushion to keep squeezing it higher — a disappointing print or
just short-term profit-taking could give back the month's gain fast, and
because the superinvestor buying is long-horizon and fundamentals-driven, it
offers no near-term price support if that reversal happens.
