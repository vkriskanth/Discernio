## Thesis

NVDA's 12-month tape (+23.1%) masks a two-speed year: the move front-loaded into the 6-9 month window, stalled to +1.5% over the trailing quarter, then reignited with a +5.7% 1-month pop — a pause-and-resume pattern, not exhaustion. RSI14 at 61 and 15.2% above the 200DMA say the stock is extended but nowhere near overbought, leaving room to run before technicals force a reset. Short interest is a non-factor here — 1.3% of float, 2.2 days to cover — so the score's 6.3 squeeze subscore is correctly near-zero; any further upside has to come from spot buying, not covering, and the 0.68 put/call OI ratio confirms options positioning is only mildly call-skewed rather than euphoric. The superinvestor tape is the real tell: ten buy/add filings across Q1-Q2 2026 spanning growth (Tiger Global, Egerton Capital) and deep-value shops (Century Management, Pzena, Davis Advisors) that don't typically chase a name up 23% YoY and trading at 34x trailing — with Duan Yongping's 91% add taking NVDA to 5.76% of his book. That's cross-style conviction buying into strength, which historically extends trend more than late-cycle momentum chasing does. Fundamentals backstop the tape: 85% revenue growth and a forward P/E of 17.3 versus 34.3x trailing imply the market is pricing continued earnings compounding rather than pure multiple expansion, which lowers — but doesn't eliminate — mean-reversion risk at these levels.
