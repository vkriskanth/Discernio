## Thesis

PDD is a broken trend dressed up as a value-buyer's darling: -2.8% 1m, -11.85% 3m,
-15.93% 6m, and -27.76% 12m, with price still 17.2% below the 200DMA — every
window is negative and the stock has not printed a technical bottom. RSI14 at
48.4 sits dead in neutral territory, so there's no oversold spring loaded here;
this is a name drifting lower on trend, not one coiled for a mean-reversion
bounce. Short interest is thin (4% of float, 5.1 days to cover), which cuts
both ways — it confirms the decline isn't short-driven manipulation, but it
also means there's no squeeze mechanism to force a violent reversal if
sentiment turns. What actually moves the needle is the buy-side conviction
stacked into the weakness: Li Lu added 133.5% to a position that now sits at
12.68% of Himalaya Capital's portfolio, and Duan Yongping (H&H) added in back-
to-back quarters (Q1 and Q2 2026), both signature value investors doubling
down as the tape worsens. That's a classic value-vs-momentum standoff — cheap,
quality-backed accumulation running directly against a still-intact downtrend
— and until price reclaims the 200DMA, the trend, not the buyers, is in
control.

## Key risk

Value trap: with short interest too low to fuel a squeeze and RSI showing no
capitulation extreme, there's no technical mechanism forcing a reversal — if
the superinvestor buying doesn't show up in flows/price soon, PDD can keep
grinding to new lows below the 200DMA regardless of how much conviction Li Lu
and Duan Yongping have expressed, and the missing put/call data means we
can't independently confirm whether options positioning is already flagging
that risk.
