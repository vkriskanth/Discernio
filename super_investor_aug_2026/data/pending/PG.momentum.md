## Thesis

PG isn't trending, it's chopping: -0.12% 1m, +1.39% 3m, -7.59% 6m, -3.80% 12m, with RSI14 at 46.87 and price sitting essentially on the 200DMA (-0.70%). That's a low-beta (0.377) staple grinding sideways with no directional conviction and no mean-reversion setup either way — no oversold bounce case, no overbought fade case. Positioning offers no fuel: short interest is negligible (1.17% of float, 3.1 days to cover), so the squeeze component correctly reads near-zero, and this is a name shorts simply don't target rather than one bulls are being punished on. The put/call OI ratio of 0.585 pushes the options-skew component to 61.03, the strongest of the five inputs, but in a low-vol defensive name that reading is as likely to reflect covered-call overwriting as genuine bullish speculation, so I'd discount it as weak signal riding on top of an otherwise flat tape. The superinvestor activity — Katz's outsized 187.78% share add (1.14% of portfolio) alongside smaller Yacktman and Mairs & Power adds — looks like value managers accumulating into 6- and 12-month weakness (rev growth is only 1.5%, so this isn't a growth re-rating story), which is a fundamentals-driven signal, not a momentum-confirming one, and shouldn't be read as smart money chasing a trend that doesn't exist yet.
