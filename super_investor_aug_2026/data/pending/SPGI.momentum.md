## Thesis

SPGI is in the back half of a failed bounce: 3m (+9.70%) and 6m (+11.82%)
returns show the stock had clawed back from its 12m drawdown (-20.76%), but
the tape has rolled over hard in the last month (-8.56%), pulling price 3.0%
below the 200DMA and RSI14 down to 34.5 — oversold-adjacent but not yet a
capitulation print. What separates this from a garden-variety breakdown is
the buyer list: Dorsey, Ackman, Bloomstran, Li Lu, Hohn and Armitage all
initiated or added in Q1/Q2 2026, several as top-5 portfolio weights (Dorsey
at 7.54% new, then +42% more), which is a concentrated, valuation-anchored
crowd, not momentum tourists. Short interest is thin (2.99% of float, 3.96
days to cover) and put/call OI sits near parity (0.90), so this decline
reads as index/macro-driven derating rather than a thesis-driven short
attack — there's no crowded bear position to squeeze, but also no
structural-impairment signal in the options or borrow data. Net: near-term
trend is genuinely negative and could extend before it turns, but the
quality-investor accumulation at a still-reasonable 20.7x forward P/E and
4.4% FCF yield argues for a mean-reversion setup building underneath the
weak tape rather than a broken name.

**adjusted_score: 46** — shade up modestly from the mechanical 40.9. The
composite's short_squeeze (14.95) and options_skew (39.77) subscores
correctly show no positioning fuel either direction, but the model can't see
that the marginal buyer here is a cluster of concentrated, long-duration
compounders adding into weakness rather than chasing strength — that
demand is a floor the pure price/RSI/200DMA math doesn't price in. Not a
full bullish flip because the 1m trend is sharply negative and RSI hasn't
reached true oversold (<30) or turned yet.

## Key risk

The 13F buys are quarter-end snapshots that could already be stale by
mid-August; if ratings/subscription revenue growth decelerates further or
rates-driven multiple compression continues, RSI grinds into the low-20s
and price breaks further below the 200DMA before any of these managers'
cost basis acts as real support — the "smart money floor" thesis needs
several more quarters to be validated and offers no protection against
near-term continuation of the down-move.
