# TJX — Momentum Note

**Thesis** — TJX's 12-month tape (+17.4%) is doing all the work; the last two
quarters have gone nowhere, with 1m/3m/6m returns of +2.1%/+2.5%/+0.8% and
price parked 0.6% under the 200DMA — a stalled uptrend consolidating rather
than extending. RSI14 at 50.2 confirms there's no directional impulse left in
either direction, and at 0.62 beta this was never going to be a momentum name
to begin with — it's a low-volatility off-price retailer trading on its
defensive characteristics, not on a squeeze or a breakout. Short interest is
negligible (1.76% of float, 3.89 days to cover), so there's no positioning
tension to resolve — this stock isn't being fought by anyone. The put/call OI
ratio of 0.76 leans mildly call-heavy but is close enough to neutral to add
little conviction either way. Jensen Investment Management's Q2 2026 add
(+2072.6% share change, now 1.76% of their book) is a quality-compounder
signal, not a momentum catalyst — Jensen buys durable franchises on
fundamentals, and that kind of sponsorship supports a floor under the
consolidation rather than arguing for a near-term breakout.

**Score adjustment**
adjusted_score: 51
The mechanical 48.7 is dragged down by the short_squeeze subscore (8.8),
which is penalizing TJX for structurally low short interest as if that were
bearish positioning — it isn't, it's just what a boring, well-owned staple
looks like; stripping that noise, the other four components average ~51.5,
and fresh high-conviction superinvestor buying nudges the fair read to 51
rather than the raw composite.

**Key risk** — This is sideways consolidation sitting right at the 200DMA
with no short-covering fuel and no options tailwind to arrest a move down; if
a soft consumer/apparel print pushes price through the 200DMA, the
already-decayed trend (component 57.96 vs. spot RSI of 50) has nothing
underneath it to stop a mean-reversion slide, and Jensen's buy is a
multi-quarter fundamental thesis, not price support that shows up this week.
