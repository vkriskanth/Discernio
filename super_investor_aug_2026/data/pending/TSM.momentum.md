**Thesis**

TSM's 79.2% 12-month return is backed by a still-intact trend (RSI14 56.5, mid-range rather than overbought) and price sitting 18.9% above its 200DMA — extended but not euphoric, with room before technical exhaustion sets in. The pace has decelerated from a 2.76%/month run-rate over the trailing 3 months to 2.09% in the last month, a mild but notable cooling that bears watching rather than a red flag on its own. Positioning tells a clean institutional-accumulation story, not a squeeze setup: short interest is negligible (0.69% of float, 2.16 days to cover), so the low short_squeeze component score is a correct read of "no contrarian fuel here," not a bearish signal. The superinvestor tape reinforces the trend rather than chasing it — Tiger Global sized TSM to 2.72% of portfolio (+49.4% shares), Polen Capital more than tripled its stake (+186.9%), Appaloosa/Tepper added another 17.5%, and Weitz Investment Management opened a fresh position, spanning both growth-momentum and value-conscious managers across Q1-Q2 2026, which argues for durability of the move rather than late-cycle crowding. The one wrinkle is a put/call OI ratio of 1.115, modestly put-heavy, showing options traders are buying some downside protection even as the stock grinds higher near highs — a hedge, not a reversal signal, but consistent with a market that respects how far the stock has already run.
